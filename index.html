Common questions

Before you book.

What do I need to get started?

Fifteen minutes and a rough idea of your numbers. Guesses are fine.

Does anything go out without me?

No. The AI drafts, and you read it and press send. Nothing leaves your business without you.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Everything is set up in your own accounts, on your own screen, with you right there.

What does it cost to keep running?

The AI tools have a monthly cost of their own: about $28 to $70, depending on which ones your Setup uses. Ongoing help from me is optional, and you can stop any time.

What if it's not for me?

Tell me within 48 hours of our first session and you get all of it back. After that, give it a real 30 days. If you're still not satisfied, tell me: I get one chance to make it right, and if I can't, you get every dollar back.

What happens to my customers' details?

They stay in your accounts. I don't keep a copy of your customer list.
